Frequently asked questions

How is my calorie target calculated?

Your starting target comes from a formula estimate based on your sex, age, height, weight and activity level, adjusted for your goal rate. After you have logged a week or more of food and weight, the weekly check-in blends that estimate with what your logged intake and weight trend actually imply. The more days you log, the more it relies on your own data. Find it under Insights.

Why does my weight "trend" differ from today's weigh-in?

Daily weight swings with water, food and salt. The trend line smooths those swings so you can see the underlying direction. Dots on the chart are your individual weigh-ins.

A barcode or food isn't found. What can I do?

Barcode and online results come from Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central, which don't contain every product. Try searching by name, or use the manual-entry button (the square-and-pencil icon) at the top of Add Food to type in the nutrition yourself. Online search needs an internet connection; the offline database works without one.

How do I sync with Apple Health?

Open Settings in the app and turn on "Sync with Apple Health", then allow the permissions. The app imports your weight history and writes weight, calories, protein, carbs and fat to Health. You can change access any time in the Health app.

How do I add the widget or the Add food shortcut?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap the plus button, and search for Toate Macros to add the widget. The "Add food" action is also available in the Shortcuts app.

Can I change the colors or app icon?

Yes. In Settings choose a color theme (including High contrast), set the appearance to light or dark, and pick an app icon.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Your data is stored on your device only. We have no account system or server. When you set up a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, the app's data comes with it. There is no separate sync or export feature at the moment.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stores. Anything you wrote to Apple Health stays in Health until you remove it there.

Is Toate Macros medical advice?

No. It provides general nutrition information and estimates. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before making major changes to your diet, especially if you have a medical condition.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Online search and barcode lookups need an internet connection.
