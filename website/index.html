Toate Macros is a fast, focused calorie and macro tracker for iPhone. Log meals in seconds, watch your real weight trend, and get calorie targets that adapt to your results.

Download on the App Store

Requires iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built around the way people actually log food: quickly, with accurate data and no friction.

Today at a glance

Rings, bars, split view and more. See calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar and sodium against your goals.

Fast food search

Search a bundled offline USDA database, plus Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central when you're online. Results are merged and ranked.

Barcode scanner

Point the camera at a packaged food to pull up its nutrition in a moment.

Real weight trend

Daily weigh-ins are noisy. A smoothed trend line shows what your body is actually doing.

Targets that adapt

A weekly check-in blends a formula estimate with your logged intake and weight trend to refine your calorie target.

Recipes and templates

Save the meals you eat often as recipes or templates.

Apple Health

Optionally import your weight history and write weight, calories and macros back to Health.

Widget and Shortcuts

Keep today's macros on your Home Screen and jump straight to adding food.

Make it yours

Six color themes including a high contrast option, light or dark appearance, kg or lb, and two app icons.
