Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

In short: Toate Macros has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your food log, weight and settings are stored on your iPhone. We do not collect your personal data and we cannot see it.

Who we are

Toate Macros ("the app", "we") is an iPhone calorie and macro tracker. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it. If you have questions, contact us at octavian.mihai321@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following on your iPhone only:

  • Foods you log, meals, recipes and templates.
  • Weight entries.
  • Your profile and goals (sex, age, height, activity level, goal rate, macro preferences) used to calculate targets.
  • App settings such as theme, units and layout.

This data is not sent to us or to any server we operate. We have no servers that receive it. Your iPhone's own backups (for example iCloud Backup or an encrypted Mac backup) may include app data, under your Apple account and settings. Apple, not us, controls that.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on "Sync with Apple Health", the app asks permission to:

  • Read your body weight history, to import it.
  • Write body weight, dietary energy (calories), protein, carbohydrates and total fat that you log.

Health data is used only to provide these features inside the app. We do not use it for advertising or marketing, and we do not share it with anyone. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app or in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

Camera (optional)

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are not stored or sent anywhere. The decoded barcode number is used to look up the product (see below).

Food lookups over the network

The app includes an offline food database. When you are online it can also search two public food databases:

  • Open Food Facts: receives your search text or scanned barcode.
  • USDA FoodData Central: receives your search text.

These requests do not include your name, log, weight, profile or any identifier we assign (we assign none). Like any internet request, they reveal your IP address to those services, which handle it under their own privacy policies. If you prefer to avoid this, use the offline database or enter foods manually.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Widget and Shortcuts

The Home Screen widget and the "Add food" shortcut share today's totals with the app through an on-device App Group. This data does not leave your iPhone.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Your choices and deleting your data

You control your data. Delete individual entries inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. Data you chose to write to Apple Health stays in Health until you delete it there. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot retrieve or delete it for you.

Not medical advice

Toate Macros provides general nutrition information and is not a medical device. Consult a qualified professional before changing your diet.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
